# tip: the hover box on a setting in Settings. The setting (its key, its name, or tNN for the browser's own word number NN), then what it does
tip	Wallpaper	A picture of yours behind Speed Dial. Right-click Speed Dial to change it quickly.
tip	control	A short animation plays when Capri takes the wheel to do something on a page for you.
tip	discordmsg	Capri can send Discord messages for you in the browser. She always asks before anything is sent.
tip	capmenu	The right-click menu on pages in Capri's colours, with her own options at the top. Turn it off for Edge's usual menu.
tip	lookask	When a question needs the web, Capri asks if she should look it up first. Off: she just looks it up.
tip	researchmenu	Highlight words on a page and right-click for Let Capri research this.
tip	watch	Say "watch this page" or "tell me when the price drops". Capri checks up to 5 pages every 20 minutes while Capri is running, for up to 2 weeks.
tip	formfill	Say "fill in this form with ...". Capri never fills in passwords, card, bank or ID numbers, outlines what she filled, and asks before sending.
tip	readdiscord	With Discord open and signed in on a tab here (not the Discord app), Capri says who messaged you and only reads it out if you say yes. To reply she repeats your exact words and sends them only on another yes. Login codes are never read out.
tip	readwhatsapp	With WhatsApp Web open and signed in on a tab here, Capri says who messaged you (not muted chats) and only reads it out if you say yes. To reply she repeats your exact words and sends them only on another yes. Login codes are never read out.
tip	skipask	When an ad, intro or recap can be skipped, Capri asks first, then presses Skip if you say yes.
tip	mouse	When Capri clicks on a page, your real mouse pointer moves there so you can see what she's doing.
tip	catchup	Come back to a tab that changed a lot after 3 minutes or more, and Capri asks if you want to know what's new. Never on video sites.
tip	watchalong	While you watch a video, Capri chimes in with a reaction now and then.
tip	watchoften	Watch-along reactions come more often.
tip	autofull	Videos Capri opens for you start in full screen.
tip	react	When a video finishes, Capri says what she thought of it.
tip	askmenu	Select words on a page, right-click, and pick Ask Capri about this.
tip	glitch	A glitchy flicker over the screen when Capri takes over.
tip	hoard	Capri teases you when more than 20 tabs are open.
tip	achieve	Little pop-ups when you unlock a browser achievement.
tip	scam	Capri warns you when a site looks like a scam or a fake sign-in page.
tip	shieldtalk	When the shield stops a sneaky redirect or pop-up, Capri sometimes says something about it.
tip	downloads	Capri warns you when a download is a program, since programs can change your PC.
tip	darkweb	Turns bright web sites dark, even ones that have no dark mode of their own.
tip	timed	Wiki races get a 3-minute timer.
tip	boot	Capri Browser's start-up animation when it opens.
tip	sleep	Tabs you haven't used for 20 minutes are put to sleep to save memory. They wake up when you open them.
tip	autopip	Switch away from a playing video and it pops out into a small window that stays on top.
tip	Capri's hologram when she talks	Capri's call screen from Holocall, a mod by Issei, shows in the corner of the browser while she talks. Get Holocall from the Capri app's mod browser. Its sounds don't play here.
tip	t12	The typeface used on Capri Browser's own pages, like Speed Dial and Settings.
tip	t13	How big the words are on Capri Browser's own pages.
tip	t33	Sites that have a light and a dark look switch along with your screen mode.
tip	t21	Shows each Speed Dial site's name above its tile.
tip	t22	The language Capri Browser's own pages use, and the one Capri answers in.
tip	t34	Where searches from the address bar and Speed Dial go.
tip	t36	Stops sites following you around the web. Strict blocks the most, but may break some sites.
tip	t43	The folder your downloads are saved in.
tip	t46	When Capri Browser opens, the tabs you had open last time come back.
