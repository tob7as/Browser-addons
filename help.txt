Ask Capri in chat, for example:
• "open YouTube" or "open the Roblox website"
• "find me a funny video to watch"
• "full screen" or "pop the video out"
• "skip 30 seconds", "play at 1.5x", "go to 3:20"
• "next video", "turn on captions", "loop it"
• "look up ..." to get an answer from the web
• "summarise this page" or "what does this page say about ..."
• "find this cheaper" or "any coupon codes for this site?"
• "take a screenshot and put it in my notes"
• "click ...", "scroll down", "go back"
• "show me something random" or "find a random website"
• "switch to my YouTube tab", "close this tab", "open a new tab"
• "type hello in the search box" then "press enter"
• "play the newest" for the latest upload from a channel or show
• "find me a Roblox game" (or Steam, itch.io, anything)
• "open my Discord in my browser and message Sam hi"
• "bookmark this" to save the page to Speed Dial
• "add to my notes ...", "note this down", "write that down"
• "research this" or "look into ...": she looks it up and saves it as a note
• "let's play snake", "play wiki race", "quiz me"
• "read this page to me" or "translate this page"
• "what's new in Capri Browser?" for the latest update notes

In the browser:
• Highlight text, right-click, then Ask Capri about this
• YouTube ads are skipped automatically
• Games, stats and achievements are on the sidebar
• The ≡ menu has screenshot to notes, dark mode for sites and pop-out video
• Right-click a tab and choose Add to bookmarks
• Free Games This Week is on the Games page
• Settings > Update all gets new games and lists right away (it also checks once a day)
%%
≡  the menu: zoom, print, screenshot, translate...
Right-click a tab  duplicate, mute, close others
Ctrl+T  new tab
Ctrl+Shift+N  private tab
Ctrl+W  close tab (or middle-click it)
Ctrl+Shift+T  reopen a closed tab
Ctrl+Tab  next tab
Ctrl+L  type in the bar
Ctrl+F  find on the page
Ctrl+Plus / Ctrl+Minus  zoom
Ctrl+P  print
Ctrl+H  history
Ctrl+J  downloads
F5  reload
F11  full screen
Ctrl+D  add this site to Speed Dial
Ctrl+Shift+S  Capri sums up the page
Ctrl+Shift+A  ask Capri about the page (or type ?question in the bar)
Ctrl+M  mute or unmute the tab
Alt+Left / Alt+Right  back / forward
