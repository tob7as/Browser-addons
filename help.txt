Just ask Capri in chat, in your own words. Here's what she can do:

VIDEOS
• "find me a funny video" or "find me an action anime": she picks one that fits and plays it
• "play the newest" for the latest upload from a channel or show
• "pause", "skip 30 seconds", "go to 3:20", "play at 1.5x", "next video", "turn on captions", "loop it"
• "full screen" or "pop the video out"

BROWSING
• "open YouTube" or "open the Roblox website"
• "click ...", "scroll down", "go back", "type hello in the search box" then "press enter"
• "switch to my YouTube tab", "close this tab", "open a new tab", "move this tab left"
• "summarise this page", "read this page to me", "translate this page"
• "what does this page say about ..." or "look up ..." for a quick answer
• "show me something random" or "find a random website"
• "bookmark this" to save the page to Speed Dial
• "find me a YouTube logo" or "find me an anime pfp": picture results, with safe search on
• "split screen" (or right-click a tab) shows two tabs side by side; tabs you haven't used for 20 minutes snooze to save memory

CAPRI RESEARCH (Settings > Research)
• "research ..." or "look into ...": she reads several sites while you carry on (in hidden views of her browser when it's open, out of your tabs and history)
• Safe search is always on, and she won't research adult or dangerous topics
• "what's the best ..." for top picks, or "what's the best website for ..."
• "fact check ..." or "is it true that ..."
• "compare my tabs", "which of these should I buy?" or "what have I got open?"
• Put a question, a screenshot or a file in Noted, then say "work out my last note" or "work out my School notes" (or right-click a note > Work on this)
• "watch this page" or "tell me when the price drops": she checks every 20 minutes
• "any new changes on my page?": she checks the pages she's watching right now
• "fill in this form with ...": never passwords or card details, and she asks before sending
• When she's done she asks: bring it up, put it in your notes, or both?
• "bring up my research" or "add my research to my notes" for the last thing she found

NOTES
• "add to my notes ...", "note this down", "write that down"
• "take a screenshot and put it in my notes"
• "summarise this page and add it to my notes"

CUSTOM JOBS
• "open custom jobs" (or the sidebar): your own commands, like "movie night", with a link and steps in whatever words you like
• A step can be any of her commands, "check the comments", "ask Ready?" (waits for your yes) or "wait for it to load"; give a job a few names: "movie night / film time"
• "make a job called movie night: open youtube.com, full screen", "what jobs do I have?", "delete my movie night job"

SHOPPING
• "find this cheaper" or "any coupon codes for this site?"
• "find me a Roblox game" (or Steam, itch.io, anything)

MESSAGES (Settings > Discord, off until you turn them on)
• "any new Discord messages?": she says who messaged, reads it on your yes, and can reply for you
• "message Alex on Discord saying ...": she types it and asks before sending
• She never sends anything without your yes

GAMES
• "let's play snake", "play wiki race", "quiz me"
• In a wiki race, press HELP or say "give me a hint"; pick Easy, Hard or Challenging on the Games page
• Games, stats and achievements are on the sidebar, and Free Games This Week is on the Games page
• Leaderboards: add a leaderboard name to join the weekly top 100s; everyone starts again from 0 every Monday
• All-time stats are only for you: they never reset and are never sent
• Featured boxes at the top of the Games page (can be turned off in Settings)

IN THE BROWSER
• Highlight text and right-click: Ask Capri about this, or Let Capri research this
• Come back to a tab that changed and she catches you up
• The ≡ menu has screenshot to notes, dark mode for sites and pop-out video
• Right-click a tab and choose Add to bookmarks; drag tabs to reorder them
• Press Esc any time to stop her taking control
• History has a search box: delete the last 7 days, 30 days, or right-click one page to delete it
• Extensions: open the Chrome Web Store and press Add to Capri Browser, or add .zip and .crx files
• The ≡ menu > Useful information has the welcome tips, plus Report a bug and Suggest an idea

IN SETTINGS
• Capri features: watch-along reactions, the takeover animation, and moving the mouse
• Research: how hard she looks, asking before looking things up, watching pages, filling in forms
• Video player: offer to skip ads, intros and recaps (she asks first), and the Anime and Video sites she uses
• Appearance: 20 themes and the Capri-styled right-click menu
• Update all installs every game and language that came with your version
• "what's new in Capri Browser?" for the latest update notes
