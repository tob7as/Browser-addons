CAPRI BROWSER

A full web browser that Capri can use alongside you.

GET STARTED >

Set up video playback
Video and most modern sites use the Microsoft Edge engine. Select the button below once to set it up.
Personalise
Add sites to Speed Dial with +, and pick one of 20 themes, a search engine and your language in Settings.
Ask Capri
Ask Capri in chat to open sites, find videos or look things up, in your own words. She uses this browser to do it. Press Esc any time to take back control.
GAMES >
13 games built in, with weekly leaderboards, stats and achievements. Select here to open the Games page, or just say let's play snake.
Daily Word Search
A new puzzle every day, the same one for everyone, from 140 themes. Every third day is a HARD one: a bigger grid, more words and decoy letters. Keep your streak going.
Play with Capri
Wiki Race, Quiz, Guess the Site, Typing Race (with Survival mode), Guess the Anime Character, Guess the Celebrity and Minecraft with Capri. In Wiki Race, press HELP or say give me a hint.
Quick games
Neon Snake, Memory Match, Reaction Test and Site Roulette, plus Free Games This Week for the games that are free to keep right now.
Leaderboards
Pick a name to join the weekly top 100. Everyone starts again at 0 every Monday. Only your name and your scores are sent.
Stats and achievements
This week's stats, all-time stats that are never reset and never sent, and 44 achievements to unlock, including what Capri has helped you with.
Game mode
Playing a PC game? The game mode button on the sidebar makes Capri Browser go super low so your game gets the PC. Select it again to turn it off.

EXAMPLE REQUESTS >

Video
find me a funny video, play the newest one, full screen, pop the video out, skip 30 seconds, subtitles on
Browsing
open YouTube, click on ..., scroll down, switch to my YouTube tab, split screen, translate this page
Reading
summarise this page, read this to me, what does this page say about ...?, or highlight text and right-click Ask Capri
Capri Research
research ..., what's the best ...?, fact check ..., keep researching it. She reads several sites while you carry on. Choose short, mid or long write-ups in Settings > Research.
Watching pages
keep an eye on this page, tell me when the price drops. She checks every 20 minutes and tells you what changed.
Shopping and safety
find this cheaper, any coupon codes?, compare my tabs, is this site safe?
Notes
add to my notes ..., take a screenshot and put it in my notes. Put a question or file in Noted and say solve my last note.
Custom jobs
Your own commands, like movie night: a link and steps in your own words. Say make a job called movie night: open youtube.com, full screen.
Discord messages
do I have any unread messages?, message Alex on Discord saying .... Turn it on in Settings > Discord and sign in to Discord here once.

ON THE SIDEBAR >

Bookmarks and history
Bookmarks (Ctrl+Shift+B), and History (Ctrl+H) with a search box. Delete the last 7 or 30 days, or right-click a page to delete it.
Noted and Capri Cleaner
Noted keeps your notes, screenshots and research. Capri Cleaner frees up space and memory.
Passwords
A password manager that stays on this PC, locked with Windows encryption. Import from Chrome, Edge, Opera or Firefox, and it offers to fill a saved login for you.
Extensions
Open the Chrome Web Store and select Add to Capri Browser, or add .zip and .crx files.
Handy keys
Ctrl+T new tab, Ctrl+Shift+N private tab, Ctrl+Shift+T reopen a closed tab, Ctrl+F find, Ctrl+L type an address. Tabs you haven't used for 20 minutes sleep to save memory.
What's new
It opens by itself after an update, or say what's new in Capri Browser? any time.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY >

Microsoft Edge engine
Pages render in WebView2 and receive the same security updates as Edge.
Your accounts
Passwords and sign-ins are stored locally on this PC. Capri never reads or enters passwords or payment details, and she asks before sending any form.
No telemetry
The mod collects no data. Page content is only sent to Capri when you ask her about a page. Leaderboards and the bug box only send what you choose to send.
Private tabs
Private tabs keep no history and are never read by Capri.
Discord stays yours
Every Discord setting is off until you turn it on. Capri only reads a message out after you say yes, and never sends anything without asking first.
Protection
Blocks ads and trackers (like Google Analytics and the Meta pixel), stops pop-ups and sneaky redirects, and warns about suspicious sites and program downloads. Each one can be turned off in Settings.
Checked add-ons
Games, themes and lists are checked against fingerprints built into the mod, so nobody can swap them. Games run locked down, away from your tabs.
Readable source
The mod's source code is included and can be reviewed.
