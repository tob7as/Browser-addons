CAPRI BROWSER

A full web browser that Capri can use alongside you.

GET STARTED >

Set up video playback
Video and most modern sites use the Microsoft Edge engine. Select the button below once to set it up.
Personalise
Add sites to Speed Dial with +, and choose a theme and search engine in Settings.
Ask Capri
Ask Capri in chat to open sites, find videos or look things up. She uses this browser to do it.

EXAMPLE REQUESTS >

Video
find me a funny video, full screen, pop the video out, skip 30 seconds, louder
Reading
summarise this page, read this to me, scroll for me, or highlight text and right-click Ask Capri
Shopping and safety
find this cheaper, any coupon codes?, is this site safe?, what was that site about ...
Discord messages
do I have any unread messages?, message Alex on Discord saying .... Turn it on in Settings > Discord and sign in to Discord here once.
Notes and history
add to my notes ..., research this, take a screenshot and put it in my notes. History has a search box, and right-click a page to delete it.
Extras
show me something random, plus games, stats, achievements and weekly leaderboards on the sidebar

PRIVACY AND SECURITY >

Microsoft Edge engine
Pages render in WebView2 and receive the same security updates as Edge.
Your accounts
Passwords and sign-ins are stored locally in the browser profile. Capri never reads or enters passwords or payment details.
No telemetry
The mod collects no data. Page content is only sent to Capri when you ask her about a page. Leaderboards and the bug box only send what you choose to send.
Private tabs
Private tabs keep no history and are never read by Capri.
Discord stays yours
Every Discord setting is off until you turn it on. Capri only reads a message out after you say yes, and never sends anything without asking first.
Protection
Ad blocking, plus warnings for suspicious sites and program downloads. Each feature can be turned off in Settings.
Readable source
The mod's source code is included and can be reviewed.
