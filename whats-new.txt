Capri Browser 2.0.2
• Settings, sorted out: new groups, Notifications and Games have their own sections, and a search box at the top left finds any setting as you type.
• Capri's page: a made-up profile that's new every day, with her finds, story, mood, guestbook and shelf. Say "what have you been looking at?" or use the sidebar button (hide it in Sidebar setup). Say "you'd like this" on a page to give it to her. It all stays on your PC.
• Smarter custom jobs: say "listen for skip = scroll" so she reacts to you while a job runs, "repeat 20 times" or "repeat forever", and "wait 10 to 20 seconds". "put on something chill" really searches and plays something. Esc always stops a job, and she tells you about any step she couldn't do.
• Don't use my mouse (Settings > Capri features): jobs run in their own tab and leave your mouse alone, so you can do other things.
• Open with Capri (Settings > Start-up & tabs, off until you turn it on): the browser opens when Capri starts. Turn on Start with Windows in Capri too and it opens with your PC.
• Windows Hello (Settings > Privacy & security, off until you turn it on): asks for your PIN, face or fingerprint before a saved password is shown, copied or filled.
• Backup and restore: save your notes, bookmarks, Speed Dial, jobs and settings to a file, never your passwords (Settings > Cleaner & backup).
• Hide Capri in full screen (Settings > Video player, off until you turn it on): move the mouse and a button on the side of the screen brings her back.
• Research card: shows what she's up to while she researches.
• Crash recovery: if a page or the browser's engine stops, it can be brought back.
• Safer password vault: more checks on what is kept and filled.
• Capri Cleaner moved: it's now in Settings > Cleaner & backup. Its old side panel is gone, and every choice is still there.

Capri Browser 2.0.1
• Games are front and centre: every game shows on the Games page straight away, no downloading first. New: Daily Word Search, a new puzzle every day that's the same for everyone, from 140 themes, and every third day is a hard one. Keep your streak going and share your time.
• Ad blocker stats: see how many ads, trackers and pop-ups Capri has stopped, on All-time stats.
• Sketchy downloads: if a download looks dodgy (a program from a site she doesn't know, or a file pretending to be a picture or document), Capri asks if you're sure first.
• Game mode can switch on by itself when a full-screen game starts (Settings > Games).
• Notifications: turn all of Capri's notifications off with one switch, or just the ones you don't want (Settings > Notifications).
• Research (Settings > Research): choose how much she writes, Short, Mid or Long. When she is done she asks if you want her to keep researching, and you can say "keep researching" any time to go deeper. Big brains will be better: a bigger AI model writes better research.
• Stats: Daily Word Search in This Week and All-time stats, and a new Capri Helped You section in All-time stats (research, jobs, messages read out, skips). 27 new achievements to unlock.
• Leaderboards: choose which of your numbers go on the boards. Set a new best and Capri tells you where it would place this week.
• Code Breach and Mystery Choom have been retired to make room.
• Useful information (on the sidebar) has a new Games section, select it to jump straight to the Games page, plus more on highlight and right-click, Research, watching pages, custom jobs, the sidebar, passwords and handy keys.
• Wallpapers: right-click any picture on a page and pick Make wallpaper. Every wallpaper is now fitted and smoothed to your screen, and Change wallpaper lists your newest pictures first (including .jpe and .jfif ones the old picker hid).
• Speed Dial: the pencil on a tile now lets you Change image, Edit name or Edit link. The picture and name stay with the tile when you change its link.
• Themes reach everything: Settings and message notifications now change with your colour theme, and the font and text size you pick in Settings > Appearance work on all of Capri Browser's own pages.
• Your sites: "find me an anime" and "find me a video" now always use your Anime site and Video site (custom links too) unless you name another one, and she no longer types "picked" into the search box.
• Jobs stay put: switch tabs while Capri works and she carries on in her tab, and she leaves the mouse alone while you're using it.
• Smarter custom jobs: write a job the way you'd say it ("can you open the website then look for prices and then summarise it and screenshot it and put it into my work notes") and she splits it into steps, both when she runs it and with Capri learn. Capri learn keeps every line you wrote, "put it in my work notes" sends the summary and screenshot to that notebook, and "open any website" opens one from your Speed Dial or a random article.
• Pick for me: a job step like "click any game" or "pick a random video" makes her choose one at random from the page.
• More extensions work: parts only for the browser itself (developer tools, side panels, bookmarks, history, downloads...) are left out instead of the whole extension being turned away, so ones like PreMiD go in. She tells you what was left out. If an extension tries to open Edge's own Extensions page (like PreMiD's "Allow User Scripts"), you get Capri Browser's extensions panel instead of an error page.
• Summarise scan: the Summarise button now shows her reading scan on the page, like jobs do.
• Tracker blocker: stops more trackers (like the Facebook and LinkedIn pixels), counts Google Analytics and the like as trackers, and works from the start, even if you never pressed Update all.
• Fixes: Websites can no longer fake game scores or leaderboard results. Capri reminds you to open Capri Browser from Play after an update. Discord message cards type out the whole message again. Update all has moved off the Games page: it's in Settings > Updates.

Capri Browser 2.0 (needs Capri 0.9.4 or newer)
• New: split screen (right-click a tab), tabs that glide when you move them, and tabs you leave for 20 minutes snooze with a moon on them. Say "find me a YouTube logo" or "an anime pfp" for pictures, "work out my School notes" or right-click a note > Work on this, and "any new changes on my page?". Esc stops her straight away.
• Capri Research (Settings > Research): say "research ...", "what's the best ...", "best website for ...", "fact check ...", "compare my tabs" or "work out my last note" (she can read screenshots). She works in the background, then asks to bring it up, put it in your notes, or both. Highlight words and right-click for Let Capri research this. With Capri Browser open she searches and reads the sites in hidden views of her own browser, so she reads whole pages. Safe search is always on, and she won't research adult topics.
• She can watch a page and tell you when the price drops or it changes, and fill in forms with what you tell her (never passwords or card details), asking before she sends.
• The right-click menu now looks like Capri, and she understands more ways to say yes and no, in other languages too.
• Discord message reader (Settings > Discord, off until you turn it on): signed in to Discord in Capri Browser, Capri says who messaged you and reads it out only if you say yes. She repeats your reply word for word and sends it only on another yes.
• Leaderboards: weekly top 100s for hours watched, videos, sites, survival, Guess the Site and typing, starting again from 0 every Monday. Pick a name on the Games page to compete.
• Found a bug or got an idea? Write it at the bottom of the Getting started page and send it to GitHub.
• Smoother: tabs fade in when you switch, closing things slides away quicker, and buttons press in.
• A much stronger ad blocker: tens of thousands of ad servers and trackers from the free EasyList and EasyPrivacy lists, ad boxes hidden on pages, invisible layers that open ads when you click are taken away, and pop-ups and sneaky redirects from clicking on nothing are stopped. Fake Download buttons get a red outline and a warning.
• Click the shield for Privacy Protection: ads, trackers and pop-ups stopped on the page, each with Turn off for this site, and the list of blocked trackers.
• Scam pages that say your PC is infected and to call a number: Capri warns you and offers to close them.
• Offer to skip (Settings > Capri features, off until you turn it on): when Skip ad, Skip intro or Skip recap shows up, Capri asks, and on your yes the mouse goes over and presses it. She never skips by herself any more.
• Say "skip 2 minutes", "go back 30 seconds", "skip to 3:20", "next video", "skip the ad" or "go back to the last video".
• The real mouse moves to what Capri clicks, skips or plays, with the takeover animation. Turn it off in Settings > Capri features.
• "Reopen that tab" brings back the last tab you closed, and the menu has Recently closed.
• Notes are a full notes app: make your own notebooks (Shopping, School, Game ideas...), search, and see when each note changed. Capri uses your notebooks and asks first: "Should I add it to Shopping notes, choom?"
• "Summarise this page and add it to my notes" does both, saving the summary with the page name, link and date. Works for "find the price and put it in my Shopping notes" too.
• Custom jobs: make your own commands with a link and steps in your own words, like "movie night". Any of her commands works as a step, "check the comments" reads just that part, "ask Ready?" waits for your yes. Press Capri learn on a job and her AI learns it, fixes your spelling and rewords it so she can do it. A cooler page with step ideas to click, or just say "make a job called movie night: open youtube.com, full screen".
• Quick answers right away: "what's 20% of 85", "what time is it in Tokyo", "5 km in miles".
• "What did I miss?" sums up what changed on a tab you left open. "Remind me about this tonight" brings the page back later.
• Watch-along (off until you turn it on): Capri reacts now and then while you watch.
• Voice commands work in 22 more languages.
• The mod is readable code now, so Capri can check it before you install it.

Capri Browser 1.9.9
• Guess the Anime Character: a popular character appears blurred and slowly sharpens. Pick the right name from four, faster for more points. Hint shows the anime for half points. Three lives. Find it on the Games page.
• Guess the Celebrity: a famous face from music, film, sport, the internet or history, blurred. Same rules, and the hint shows what they're known for.
• Mystery Choom: Capri picks a character from Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 1 or 2. Ask yes or no questions (season, hair, shades, mask...) and find them in as few as you can.
• Free Games This Week: every game you can claim and keep for free right now, from Epic, Steam, GOG, itch.io, consoles and mobile, with how long each deal has left.
• Site Roulette has twice as many sites, and Wiki Race has 50 more articles.
• Say "add to my notes ..." (or "note this down", "write that down") and Capri saves it as a new note. Say "research this" or "look into ..." and she looks it up and saves what she found as a note.
• Discord in the browser (turn it on in Settings > Capri features): "message Sam on Discord hi", "reply to Sam ...", "call Sam", "open Minecraft general", "join Minecraft gaming vc". She finds them and asks before she sends, calls or joins: say yes to go ahead.
• Capri plays what she finds: she opens the episode or presses play, and asks "want me to put it on?" if it won't start. Say "play the newest" for the newest upload.
• Ads you can't skip on YouTube are muted and fast-forwarded.
• "Find me a Roblox game" (or a Steam game, an itch.io game, or anything else): she searches, scrolls and picks one for you.
• "Find a random website" plays Site Roulette, and you can start any game by asking: "let's play snake", "play wiki race", "quiz me".
• "Bookmark this" saves the page to your Speed Dial. Also in a tab's right-click menu.
• New games: Neon Snake, Memory Match and Code Breach.
• On messaging sites like Discord, Gmail or WhatsApp, Capri asks before she clicks, types or presses Enter for you.
• Games, help and languages are only used if they exactly match the versions that shipped with your copy of Capri Browser. New ones come with a new version of the mod.
• The Welcome page has Download browser (everything at once, with progress, each file checked) or Download quietly in the background. The browser works before either finishes.
